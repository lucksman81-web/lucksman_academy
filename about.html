About Us

Lucksman's Sports Academy

Our academy is powered by some of the best coaches in the state and each individual student is given special attention when it comes to their training. We identify our student's potential and hone their skills so as they shine in any competitive event, home and abroad.

Malai Sports Academy is one of the leading sports training institutes in Chennai. Located at Gowrivakkam, our facility provides the right place for young athletes to train and prepare for state-level, national, and international athletic competitions, and events.

Vision Statement

At Lucksman's sports academy, we prepare our athletes with several rounds of training, right from the moment they join with us. Our vision is to help students from all walks of life to find out their true potential and bring it out.

With the right mindset, our young athletes can reach great heights in sports and with the proper attitude towards sports, they can use their training to excel in their academic life too.

Mission Statement

At Lucksman's Sports Academy, it is our mission to identify young talent and interests. With proper coaching and training, we will guide our pupil towards a very successful sports career.

Our Philosophy

To accomplish our mission, we impart training to our students through age based training. Our classes are divided as kids training program and youth athletic program. The earlier program is to help young children identify their interests in specific sports and impart basic physical training to them. As they progress towards the youth athletic program, they will be prepared to take part in athletic events, and competitions.
